Super Methane Brothers: licences
================================

The game: Super Methane Brothers by Mark Page (Apache Software, 1993),
the GPL conversion of it (methane.sourceforge.net, version 1.4.8), the
SDL 1 port by Spot and the SDL 2 version for AmigaOS 4 by capehill,
https://github.com/capehill/smb (commit
9f1bea67044bab88028307a81dc2614119d8ce12).

- The program (source/) is under the GNU General Public License, version
  2 or (at your option) any later version (COPYING.txt), as every source
  file says.
- The graphics, maps, music and sound effects are compiled into the
  program from source/data/*.cpp and come under the same licence: Mark
  Page released the conversion, data included, under the GPL with the
  permission of Apache Software Ltd, as upstream's readme says.
- Upstream's readme also says: "THE ORIGINAL AMIGA VERSION OF SUPER
  METHANE BROTHERS IS STILL A COMMERCIAL GAME IT'S LICENCE HAS NOT
  CHANGED." This port is built from the GPL conversion only; it holds
  nothing of the original Amiga release.
- The icons (amiga/icons/) are drawn from upstream's docs/puff.gif and
  are GPL 2 or later like the game.
- The Amiga port's own files (amiga/) are GPL 2 or later, like the game.
  amiga/amiga_screens.c comes from the AmiCradle Team's Seven Kingdoms and
  Abbaye ports.

At run time the program uses GFOpenGPU's SDL2.module and SDL2_mixer.module
(SDL 2 and SDL_mixer are under the zlib licence; SDL_mixer's tracker
module player is libxmp, MIT), which are installed with GFOpenGPU and are
not part of this drawer.
